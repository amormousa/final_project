# Phase 01 -- Market Research

## 1.1 Project Overview

Plant Green is a smart agriculture and farm management platform intended
to connect agricultural monitoring, IoT technologies, farm operations,
agricultural commerce, and business services in one digital ecosystem.

The platform's strategic direction is to help farmers and agricultural
businesses monitor their farms, organize daily operations, manage
resources, access agricultural products and services, and make more
informed decisions using digital tools and data.

### Core Areas

-   Smart Farming
-   IoT & Automation
-   Farm Management
-   Agricultural E-commerce
-   Business Management
-   Data & Analytics
-   Digital Services

### Problems the Platform Aims to Address

Agricultural businesses may face challenges such as:

-   Reliance on manual farm monitoring.
-   Limited visibility into soil and plant conditions.
-   Irrigation decisions that are not informed by continuous sensor
    data.
-   Difficulty monitoring different parts of a farm from one place.
-   Disconnected workflows for tasks, maintenance, and seasonal
    planning.
-   Difficulty tracking products, orders, and inventory.
-   Separate systems for delivery and returns.
-   Limited access to organized reports and operational insights.

These are market problem hypotheses and should be validated through
interviews and research with actual farmers and agricultural businesses.

### Proposed Solution Areas

**Smart Monitoring** - Monitor relevant soil and environmental
conditions. - Collect sensor readings. - Display monitoring information
in a central dashboard. - Provide alerts when configured conditions are
met.

**Smart Irrigation** - Use soil moisture readings to support irrigation
decisions. - Identify which plants or areas require watering. - Automate
watering where the hardware and control system support it. - Keep an
irrigation activity history.

**Canopy Control** - Detect rain through a rain sensor. - Respond to
configured weather conditions. - Monitor canopy status. - Support
automated and/or manual control according to the implemented hardware.

**Digital Farm Management** - Provide a central dashboard. - Organize
seasonal activities. - Manage maintenance requests. - Present farm
performance indicators.

**Marketplace & Business Operations** - Display agricultural products. -
Support shopping and order workflows. - Manage supplier products. -
Connect inventory, shipping, and returns workflows where integrations
are available.

# Phase 02 -- Brand Strategy & Identity

## 2.1 Brand Name

**Brand Name: Plant Green**

The recommendation is to retain the current name unless user research or
legal/trademark checks reveal a strong reason to change it.

The name communicates a clear association with plants, agriculture,
growth, and sustainability.

Before commercial launch, perform: - Trademark availability checks in
the intended markets. - Domain availability checks. - Social media
handle checks. - Similar-name and brand-confusion checks.

## 2.2 Brand Story

Plant Green began with the idea that modern agriculture needs more than
separate irrigation tools or isolated ways to monitor plants. It needs a
connected system that brings together farm monitoring, technology, and
everyday management.

Through Smart Monitoring, Farm Management, and Digital Services, Plant
Green aims to help farm owners understand their farms, organize their
operations, and make decisions based on useful information.

The brand's story should communicate practical value rather than
technology for its own sake: technology should make farm operations
easier to understand and manage.

## 2.3 Logo Direction

Three possible creative directions:

### Direction A -- Leaf + Technology

A leaf combined with subtle digital elements to communicate agriculture
and technology.

### Direction B -- Smart Agriculture / Circuit + Leaf

A leaf integrated with circuit-inspired lines or connected nodes. This
is the preliminary recommended direction because it communicates both
agriculture and connected technology.

### Direction C -- Growth + Fields

A visual inspired by field rows, growth, and upward movement,
communicating productivity and development.

### Logo Design Considerations

-   Must remain recognizable at small sizes.
-   Should work in full color and monochrome.
-   Must be legible on light and dark backgrounds.
-   Should have an icon-only version.
-   Avoid overly complex circuit details.
-   Prepare horizontal, stacked, and icon-only variations.

No final logo has been approved in this strategic draft.

## 2.7 UX/UI Guidelines

### Responsive Design

-   Support desktop, tablet, and mobile.
-   Prioritize essential dashboard information on smaller screens.
-   Avoid horizontal overflow in tables.
-   Use mobile-friendly navigation.

### Arabic and English

-   Support Arabic RTL and English LTR layouts.
-   Ensure icons, directional arrows, sidebars, and forms adapt
    correctly.
-   Keep translated content natural rather than relying on literal
    translation.

### UI Components

Create consistent patterns for: - Cards. - Buttons. - Tables. - Forms. -
Modals. - Navigation. - Status labels. - Notifications. - Charts.

### System States

Every important page or component should handle: - Loading. - Empty. -
Success. - Error. - Offline or disconnected. - Permission denied.

### Notifications

Notifications should be actionable and clear. For example, a
disconnected sensor notification should explain the device status and
the next step the user can take.

## 2.8 Dashboard Design Language

Use a "Summary → Details" design pattern.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Dashboard Element       Summary                 Full Experience
  ----------------------- ----------------------- -----------------------
  Farm Performance Score  Overall score and       Detailed indicators and
                          status                  trends

  Seasonal Planner        Upcoming activities     Full calendar and task
                                                  management

  Maintenance Requests    Open request count and  Request creation and
                          status                  management

  Inventory Overview      Stock summary           Inventory details and
                                                  movements

  Orders & Returns        Recent order summary    Order and return
                                                  management

  Smart Monitoring        Current readings        Device and sensor
                                                  monitoring
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

The main dashboard should not attempt to show every detail. It should
help the user understand the current situation and navigate to the
relevant task.

## 2.9 Marketing Tone of Voice

The brand should sound:

-   Professional but simple.
-   Trustworthy.
-   Educational.
-   Helpful.
-   Solution-oriented.

### Messaging Principle

Prefer practical explanations over technical claims.

Instead of: "We use advanced IoT sensors."

Prefer: "Monitor your soil conditions and make smarter irrigation
decisions."

Instead of: "AI-powered agricultural intelligence."

Prefer: "Turn farm data into useful insights that support better
decisions."

Avoid claims about improved yields, water savings, or financial returns
unless supported by measured results.

## 2.10 Content Strategy

### Educational Content

Explain smart agriculture, soil monitoring, irrigation, water
management, and seasonal planning.

### Product Features

Introduce Plant Green features and explain how they work.

### Problem & Solution

Show common farm-management challenges and how digital tools may help
address them.

### Demonstrations

Use walkthroughs, screenshots, and videos to demonstrate the platform.

### Success Stories

Publish real customer stories only after obtaining permission and
validating the results.

### Technology

Explain IoT, automation, integrations, and analytics in accessible
language.

completion.

Priority. - Creation Date. - Assigned Technician. - Current Status. -
Completion Date.

Outgoing Stock. - Manage Stock Movements. - Support Inventory Reports.

**Proposed Workflow** 1. Add or update a product in the system
responsible for product management. 2. Synchronize product data with
Odoo. 3. Retrieve or display available stock. 4. Check inventory when an
order is created. 5. Update quantities after the relevant stock
operation is confirmed. 6. Display an inventory summary in Plant Green.

**Integration Considerations** - API Authentication. - Product ID
Mapping. - Stock Synchronization. - Error Handling. - Duplicate
Transaction Prevention. - Logging & Monitoring.

Odoo Inventory can support operations such as receipts, deliveries,
internal transfers, stock tracking, and returns. The exact integration
should be designed around the selected Odoo version, configuration, and
API capabilities.

Official references: - https://www.odoo.com/app/inventory -
https://www.odoo.com/documentation/

user.

State. - Error State. - Offline State. - Permission Denied State.

blank. It should explain the connection status and suggest the next
step.
